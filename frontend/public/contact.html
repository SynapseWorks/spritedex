SpriteDex V1 Contact

SpriteDex is currently an invited-tester pilot maintained by SynapseWorks.

Bugs and non-sensitive feedback

Use the public SpriteDex GitHub issue tracker. Screenshots are welcome as long as they do not expose private credentials or a precise location you do not want public.

Privacy or account matters

Do not put passwords, access tokens, private account details, or precise private coordinates in a public GitHub issue. A dedicated private support/privacy address is a remaining launch item and will be added before the pilot opens beyond invited testers.

Field emergencies

SpriteDex is not an emergency or navigation service. For urgent safety issues, use appropriate local emergency or land-manager channels rather than SpriteDex support.

For the closed V1 field test, the tester can report private findings directly to the project maintainer outside the public issue tracker.
