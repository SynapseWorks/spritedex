SpriteDex V1 Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 4, 2026.

SpriteDex is a small biodiversity field-journal pilot operated by SynapseWorks. Privacy is part of the field design, especially because nature observations can contain precise locations.

What SpriteDex stores

For signed-in users, SpriteDex may store your email address, display name, password hash, login sessions, organism encounters, timestamps, precise encounter coordinates, notes, photos, Regional Dex progress, and encrypted credentials needed to connect an iNaturalist account.

Location privacy

Your precise SpriteDex encounter coordinates are private account data by default. Public Region and Regional Dex endpoints do not publish your personal encounter coordinates. V1's personal field map is rendered in your browser from your own authenticated encounter data and does not send those coordinates to a third-party map provider.

iNaturalist

If you choose to connect iNaturalist and sync an encounter, SpriteDex sends the observation information and any selected evidence photo to iNaturalist under your own connected account. That copy is then governed by iNaturalist's own terms, privacy settings, community identification process, and geoprivacy rules. SpriteDex does not attempt to reconstruct obscured or private iNaturalist coordinates.

Sensitive biodiversity

SpriteDex suppresses rarity-like public presentation for protected/sensitive encounters and does not use obscured external observations to infer fine-scale Region membership.

Infrastructure and logs

Application logs record operational information such as request path, status, duration, and a request ID. They are intentionally designed not to record request bodies, query strings, authentication tokens, uploaded filenames, or GPS coordinates. Private media may be stored in a private object-storage bucket.

Sharing and sale

SpriteDex does not sell personal data. V1 does not run advertising or provide advertisers access to field-journal data.

Deletion and questions

V1 is an early pilot. For non-sensitive technical questions, use the public SpriteDex GitHub issue tracker. Do not post private account, credential, or precise-location information in a public issue. A dedicated private support/privacy contact will be published before SpriteDex opens beyond invited V1 testers.

This V1 notice is intentionally readable and describes the product as currently built. It will be reviewed again before a broader public release.
