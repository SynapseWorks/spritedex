SpriteDex V1 Pilot Terms

Last updated: September 4, 2026.

SpriteDex V1 is an early field-test release. It is designed to make biodiversity exploration playful while keeping iNaturalist and other authoritative sources responsible for scientific identification and community verification.

Use it safely

Do not trespass, enter closed trails, disturb wildlife or habitat, handle organisms you cannot safely identify, or use SpriteDex directions or game progress as a reason to approach sensitive species. Follow local laws, land-manager rules, closures, permits, and posted conditions.

Identification is not safety advice

Taxon search, community identifications, encounter tiers, notes, and educational content may be incomplete or wrong. Do not use SpriteDex to decide whether an organism is edible, medicinal, safe to touch, or safe to approach.

Observations and iNaturalist

If you connect an iNaturalist account, you authorize SpriteDex to submit the encounters you choose to sync under that account. Scientific identity and data-quality status may change later through iNaturalist's community process, and SpriteDex may reconcile its game state to those changes.

Game statistics

SpriteDex Encounter Tiers describe regional/seasonal reporting frequency in the available evidence. They are not claims of ecological abundance or conservation rarity. Conservation status is a separate concept.

Availability

V1 may change, pause, lose features, or be unavailable while the pilot is being tested. External services such as iNaturalist and hosting providers can also be temporarily unavailable; SpriteDex is designed to preserve a local encounter record when external synchronization fails.

Community conduct

Do not intentionally submit fabricated observations, abuse external services, attempt to expose another person's private locations, or use SpriteDex to locate protected species for collection, harassment, or disturbance.

Feedback

Non-sensitive bugs and feature feedback can be submitted through the public SpriteDex issue tracker. Do not include credentials, private account information, or precise private locations in public reports.

These pilot terms are a practical V1 operating notice, not a substitute for a formal legal review before a larger commercial/public launch.
